# Parent Review Page

Follow the [master design system](../MASTER.md). Select a child, then show that child's confirmed accumulation and pending items. Ownership must not be implied only by color/avatar.

Review displays original scope, standard, and amount. Give specific improvements or mark checked without posting. Payment is a separate next step requiring actual delivery or reserved custody before enabling the amount-bearing confirmation. No bulk one-click check-and-pay.

After local payment, remove the pending item and update the child view on this device. Future sync needs a distinct waiting-for-receipt state; do not fake cloud delivery today. Reprocessing a request must not pay twice.

The production parent entry has a local lock. Cross-device authorization remains pending. Prototype role switching is a preview control, not an unprotected production entry.
